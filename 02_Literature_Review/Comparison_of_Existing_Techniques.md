## Comparison of Existing Techniques

This section compares the two main privacy-preserving techniques identified from the reviewed studies. Each technique has different strengths and limitations when protecting sensitive information in big data systems.

| **Technique** | **Main Strength** | **Main Limitation** |
|---|---|---|
| k-Anonymity | Simple and practical approach for reducing re-identification risk | Re-identification may still be possible under certain attacks |
| Differential Privacy | Provides a formal mathematical privacy guarantee | Added noise may reduce data utility and model accuracy |

The reviewed studies show that both techniques involve a privacy–utility trade-off. This research therefore evaluates k-anonymity and differential privacy using the same UCI Adult Census Income dataset and consistent evaluation metrics.
