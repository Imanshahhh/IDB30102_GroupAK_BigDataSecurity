# Research Gap Analysis #

## Overview

Previous studies show that improving privacy often reduces data utility. Most studies focus on only one technique or a hybrid approach, making direct comparison difficult.

## Identified Research Gap

- Most studies evaluate only one privacy-preserving technique.
- Different datasets and evaluation metrics reduce comparability.
- There is no common framework comparing anonymization, differential privacy, and federated learning.
- Limited guidance is available for selecting an appropriate privacy-preserving technique.

## Contribution of This Research

This research compares all three techniques using the same benchmark dataset and common evaluation metrics to provide a consistent comparative framework.
