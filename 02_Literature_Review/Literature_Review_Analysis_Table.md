# Literature Review Analysis Table

The reviewed studies were analyzed based on the privacy-preserving technique used, benchmark dataset, evaluation metrics, and reported findings.

| **Study** | **Technique** | **Dataset** | **Evaluation Metrics** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anoop et al. (2025) | k-Anonymity | UCI Adult | Accuracy, Re-identification Risk |
| Anoop et al. (2025) | Differential Privacy | UCI Adult | Accuracy, Re-identification Risk |
| Shathi et al. (2026) | Mondrian k-Anonymity + DP-CTGAN | UCI Adult | NCP, Privacy Budget |
| Chen et al. (2025) | Personalized Differential Privacy | Census, Graph Data | RMSE, ARE |

The reviewed studies provide supporting evidence for the comparison of k-anonymity and differential privacy. The studies use different variants, datasets, and evaluation metrics, highlighting the need for a consistent evaluation framework. This research addresses this gap by applying standard k-anonymity and standard differential privacy to the same UCI Adult Census Income dataset and evaluating both techniques using classification accuracy, re-identification risk, and execution time.
